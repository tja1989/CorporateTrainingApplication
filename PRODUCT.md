# xprtn

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Frontline retail learners, their assigned managers, and HR/training administrators. Learners often use phones and employee IDs rather than corporate email. Managers monitor and assign training for direct reports; administrators author content and review assessments, HR tickets and compliance.

## Product Purpose

Make assigned training easy to find, resume and complete; provide course-grounded tutoring and policy-grounded HR assistance. Support human-reviewed assessments and auditable compliance.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing37 page routes and all domain workflows are retained. Five lesson types, seven assessment types, learning paths, required-training rules, certificates/recertification, human review, integrity monitoring, daily optional practice, text/voice tutor and HR help, tickets, six reports, CSV import and exports. Existing Next.js/React/Tailwind/Drizzle/Postgres stack. English UI with multilingual content and RTL readiness. Offline/demo states must be labeled and cannot be claimed as live integration qualification.

## Brand Commitments

Product name is **xprtn** (lowercase); LuLu may remain as the organization/client, not product branding. User rejected both the old look and navigation. Confirmed replacement: Coursera-inspired discovery/course detail blended with LinkedIn Learning-style lesson workspace, light white/blue professional learning UI. Dark mode retained.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing repository content/schema/domain tests; fictional demo retail material and demo accounts. Full approved contract in docs/WELEARN_REWORK_PLAN.md. Do not fabricate ratings, instructors, learner numbers or testimonials.

## Product Principles

- The next learning action is immediately clear and easy to resume.
- Training urgency is visible without overwhelming course content.
- Roles, consent, finality and permission boundaries stay truthful.
- All workflows work on small screens and by keyboard.
- Browser evidence proves completion; visual screenshots alone do not.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG2.2AA target,44px product targets, reduced motion, zoom/reflow, dark mode, logical-direction layouts and readable multilingual fallback fonts.
